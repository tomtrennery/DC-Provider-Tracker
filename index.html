Confidence levels

  • Confirmed — A primary document (chair's statement, member booklet, scheme annual report) was fetched and read. The transfer is stated explicitly in that document.
  • Reported — Sourced from trade press only (Professional Pensions, Pensions Age, IPE, Corporate Adviser, etc.). Trade press alone cannot achieve Confirmed status.
  • Indicative — Indirect evidence only: a credential page, anonymised reference, a booklet URL that returned an error, or a single indirect mention. Treat with caution.

Event types

  • Bulk Transfer — all or most members and assets move from an existing arrangement to a master trust.
  • Deferred Sweep — only deferred (non-contributing) members transferred; the active section may remain in the old arrangement.
  • Contract→Trust — a stakeholder or GPP arrangement converted into a trust-based master trust.
  • MT-to-MT — one master trust consolidates into another master trust.

Ground rules

  • Public sources only — no internal or non-public information.
  • AUM in £ millions at transfer date where known, otherwise at announcement date.
  • Blank fields mean the data was not found, not that it is zero.
  • CONFLICT flag means two or more credible sources give conflicting information — see row notes.
